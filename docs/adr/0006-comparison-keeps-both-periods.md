# A comparison keeps both periods; otherwise a named year is the only window

A question that sets two periods side by side resolves to two windows. "Prices in 2024 and last month" is the whole of 2024 and last month, each its own window. A question that is not comparing periods has one window: a year written in the question, with any relative phrase in that question dropped. "Prices last month in 2024" is the whole of 2024.

The alternative was to let the explicit year always win, including in a comparison. That collapsed "2024 and last month" into 2024 and discarded the period the question asked to set beside it.
