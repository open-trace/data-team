# Plan does not replace the Question place

A Plan changes how an answer is written and what may be exported. It does not replace the Place named in the Question. A Home place fills only an empty Question place, and only when that home is itself a Place (an African country or named region).

The alternative was to treat a cheaper Plan, Farmers in particular, as a single-country retrieval clamp and to prefer the profile country over the country in the question. That made a non-African sign-in locale the geography of a Nigeria question. Pricing copy can still say a Plan is one country at a time. That sentence is answer tone. It is not the retrieval constraint.
